# Soccer Bot - Slack
Soccer Bot is a friendly Slack assistant that brings live football standings and team match schedules directly into your Slack channels!


What It Does:
Live League Tables - View current league standings for top leagues around the world.
Team Match Schedules - Look up your favorite football team to see their upcoming fixtures and recent results.

How to Use It
Once the bot is added to your Slack channel, type one of these commands into the message box:

/soccer-table PL — View current league standings (e.g., prem - Premier League, la liga - La Liga, serie a - Serie A).
/soccer-team Barcelona — Look up upcoming fixtures for any team (ex: Arsenal, Real Madrid, Barcelona).
/soccer-ping - Lets you see the ping for the bot.

Features:
Fast Responses - Get match information in seconds without leaving your chat window.
Always Available - Hosted online 24/7 so it's ready whenever you or your teammates need a quick football update.
